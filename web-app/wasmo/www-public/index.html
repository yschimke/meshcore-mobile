MeshCore

Chat over a MeshCore companion radio connected by USB.

Is this your Wasmo?

This app only opens for its owner. Sign in to your Wasmo, then open MeshCore from your launcher.

Once it's open you can install it to your desktop: use your browser's Install app.

Install MeshCore on your own Wasmo

  1. In your Wasmo, open the computer menu and choose Install App.
  2. Paste this address: https://github.com/yschimke/meshcore-mobile/releases/latest/download/meshcore.wasmo
  3. Name it meshcore and open it. A Wasmo that shares your radios across your browsers enables that by app name; under any other name they stay in that browser, and the app says so.

Connecting a radio

  • USB: works. Plug in a companion radio and choose its serial port. Needs desktop Chrome or Edge (Web Serial).
  • Bluetooth: not in the web app yet. Use the Android app.
  • Wi-Fi (TCP): browsers can't open TCP connections. Use the Android or desktop app.

Radios you've connected are remembered and, on a Wasmo that shares them, listed in every browser you use. Messages and contacts aren't saved between visits.
